# Per-user content strategy

Read this for a content calendar, performance review, or suggestions based on past results.

## Planning inputs

Use the active user's own:

- business or personal goal
- audience and topics
- desired platforms and sustainable frequency
- available media and offers
- privacy and topic boundaries
- recent content and results, when provided

Ask only for missing inputs that materially change the plan. Do not assume every user wants daily posting, viral reach, aggressive calls to action, or sales content.

## Optional 14-day plan

Store the plan at the active profile's private `content-plan.md`. A useful row contains:

```text
date/slot | platform | purpose | topic | format/media | status | result notes
```

Balance a small number of repeatable content purposes such as education, story, product/service, community question, proof/process, and light conversation. Leave rest days when appropriate. Avoid repeating the same narrative angle or visual in adjacent posts.

The plan is a draft, not authorization to publish. Every actual batch still needs the confirmation boundary in `SKILL.md`.

## Performance review

- Compare posts against that account's own recent baseline and intended purpose.
- Separate reach, engagement quality, saves/shares, replies, clicks, and downstream outcomes when data exists.
- Do not compare a community-conversation post to a sales post using one universal success number.
- Label early data as preliminary and wait for the platform's distribution to stabilize before a final judgment.
- Never claim an algorithm rule, guaranteed reach multiplier, or universal posting time without current source-backed evidence.
- Record only metrics the user provides or authorizes Codex to read from visible insights.

Use findings to adjust the next plan modestly. Preserve the user's voice and business constraints instead of chasing a single high-performing outlier.
